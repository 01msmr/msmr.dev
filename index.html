korrekturen

Reporting and tracking corrections sent to newsrooms.

Reports are submitted through an iOS Shortcut and sent as email. A SHA-256 token in the subject line serves as a correlation ID; an IMAP worker matches the outbox against the inbox and links each reply to its report. Results are reviewed in a table view with filtering and sorting.

afterworkphotos Snap Cam WebXR gallery

Square photos, taken after work — on the web, on the phone, in WebXR, straight from the camera.

On the desktop a wall of prints, on phone and iPad a pile of paper sheets to swipe through, and a WebXR gallery for VR headsets where every floor is a year, joined by a lift. Snap Cam, a personal iOS camera, takes the square shot, suggests a name and posts it; a GitHub workflow files it by date, rebuilds the index and the photo is live. No build step, no framework.

Do Day

A task and calendar PWA built on a self-hosted Nextcloud.

A Hono backend wraps CalDAV and WebDAV and exposes them as an HTTP API; the data stays in the existing Nextcloud instance. Tasks are organised with hierarchical tags following the #Area.Subarea pattern, resolved through a UID-based registry stored in WebDAV.

Chromachron

A clock without hands: fields of colour instead of numerals.

The Chromachron principle implemented in p5.js, installable as a PWA on iOS. The spiral was constructed in Rhino and carried over as a Bézier curve. Most of the effort went into iOS-specific PWA details: status bar, icon delivery and viewport behaviour.

makerspac.es

A map of makerspaces across the German-speaking world.

A directory of open workshops with filtering, clustering and radius search, born out of the Toolbox Bodensee community. Where a space publishes SpaceAPI, its opening status is shown live. An embed mode lets other sites include the map. Built without a framework, served statically from GitHub Pages.

a.msmr

A personal start page: every link one tap away.

Links in sections of equally sized tiles, with a section bar to jump between them and keyboard navigation. On iPhone and iPad the sections snap to a height grid, so none is ever cut off at the bottom edge. A signed-in edit mode sorts and nests links; a PHP check on the server flags dead ones before saving.
